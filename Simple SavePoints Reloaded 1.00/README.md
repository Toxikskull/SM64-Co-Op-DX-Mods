Simple Savepoints Reloaded v1.00

Commands:

/fullHP or /fh to get 100% HP when loading a savepoint.

/keepcoin or /kc to keep the amount of coins (Doesn't include red coins!) mario had when creating a savepoint.

/savetime or /st to keep track of level timers such as princess's secret slide. (can only be enabled by host & currently only works for PSS *no romhack levels*, also has bugs :S)

Known Issues:

-1 Reloading a save over lava with a shell will kill mario. :/

-2 reloading a save with /st /savetime enabled will sometimes reload the player in PSS/timer level on the first reload. :/
